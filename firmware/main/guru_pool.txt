# Guru habit pool.

# THIS FILE IS THE SOURCE OF TRUTH. guru_pool.c is generated from it by
# tools/gen_guru_pool.py -- edit here, then run `make -C sim gurupool`.
# Copy it to /sdcard/guru.txt to override the built-in pool on the device
# without a reflash (Guru > Menu > Export pool writes it for you).
#
# One habit per line:
#
#     id | category | name | why
#
#   id        1-64. PERMANENT. The log on the card stores this number, so a
#             record written today has to still mean the same habit next year.
#             Assign the next free one for a new habit; never reuse or renumber.
#   category  gut, metabolic, mind, strength, recovery
#   name      the row text, up to 30 characters or it clips on the device
#   why       one or two sentences, shown when the row is tapped
#
# Blank lines and lines starting with # are ignored. Order here does not
# matter -- the list is grouped by category when it is built.
#
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# NOT MEDICAL ADVICE. These are widely discussed consumer wellness practices,
# not treatment for anything. A `why` line may say what a practice is
# understood to do and why people do it -- that is what makes a habit worth
# keeping -- but it must never name a disease, promise to prevent or cure
# anything, or give a dose. "Builds aerobic base" is fine. "Prevents heart
# disease" is not. "Take 5 g" is not. Guru > Menu > About carries the
# disclaimer that ships with this list.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

4 | gut | Natto or another ferment | Fermented soybeans. Kimchi, sauerkraut or miso count for this one.
5 | gut | Kefir or live yoghurt | Live cultures rather than the sweetened, pasteurised sort.
6 | gut | A plant you have not had | Variety is the point -- people who track this aim at thirty a week.
7 | gut | Twelve hours between meals | An overnight gap. Dinner early or breakfast late, whichever you prefer.

# --- Mind --------------------------------------------------------------------
